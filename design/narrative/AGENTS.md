# Narrative Instructions

## Applies To

All files below `design/narrative/`.

## Required Practices

- Cross-reference all new lore against existing lore for contradictions.
- Mark every lore entry with canon level: Established, Provisional, or Under Review.
- Match dialogue to the character's voice profile.
- Document consistent world rules and the true answers to mysteries, even when players never learn them.
- Keep faction motivations, relationships, and power structures internally logical.
- Make narrative text localization-ready with named placeholders and avoid idioms that do not translate.
- Keep every dialogue line within 120 characters.

## Forbidden Practices

- Do not introduce uncategorized or contradictory lore.
- Do not violate a voice profile or world rule without an approved change.
- Do not use anonymous interpolation, translation-hostile idioms, or overlong dialogue.

## Verification

Run lore and world-rule consistency checks, validate canon and voice-profile metadata, test localization placeholders, and measure dialogue lines against the 120 characters limit.
